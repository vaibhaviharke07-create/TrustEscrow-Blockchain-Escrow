# TrustEscrow 🔐

### Trustless Transactions. Secure Payments. No Middleman.

TrustEscrow is a blockchain-based escrow platform designed to make online transactions between unknown buyers and sellers safer, more transparent, and reliable.

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Online transactions between unknown buyers and sellers often involve a lack of trust. Buyers worry about paying without receiving the promised product or service, while sellers worry about delivering without receiving payment.

Traditional escrow services solve this problem through centralized intermediaries, which can introduce additional fees, delays, and a single point of control.

## 💡 Our Solution

TrustEscrow uses blockchain smart contracts to securely hold the buyer's payment until the agreed transaction conditions are fulfilled.

The smart contract controls the movement of funds, allowing payment to be released, refunded, or disputed according to predefined rules.

This removes the need for a centralized middleman and provides a transparent transaction process.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Connect Wallet**
   The buyer and seller connect their crypto wallets using MetaMask.

2. **Create Escrow**
   The transaction details and parties are defined through the platform.

3. **Lock Funds**
   The buyer deposits the required funds into the smart contract.

4. **Seller Delivers**
   The seller completes the agreed work or delivers the product and marks the transaction as delivered.

5. **Release Payment**
   The buyer confirms the delivery and releases the payment to the seller.

6. **Refund or Dispute**
   If the deadline is reached or a problem occurs, the appropriate refund or dispute mechanism can be used.

7. **Transaction History**
   Completed transactions can be viewed through the platform.

## 🔐 Security

TrustEscrow uses smart-contract-based rules to control funds rather than relying on a centralized intermediary.

The smart contract includes access-control checks, transaction-state validation, events, and security practices to help prevent unauthorized actions.
